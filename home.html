📚 About JAMM

JAMM stands for Jaybhogadi, Afreen, Meghana, Mamatha — a collaborative initiative dedicated to algorithm education.

We help learners understand various algorithms through interactive visualizations that break down each step in an easy-to-learn format.

Our mission is to enable Joyful learning of Algorithms in the most effective manner.
